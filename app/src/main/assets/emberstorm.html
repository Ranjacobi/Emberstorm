The Last Ember

Long ago, the ancient star Solari burned brighter than any other in the cosmos — a beacon of warmth and light across a thousand galaxies.

But even the greatest stars must die.

When Solari collapsed, its heart shattered into a million golden sparks that scattered across the void. All that remained was a single living flame — the last ember.

You.

Now you drift through the wreckage of the cosmos. Crystal shards — the frozen bones of dead worlds — block your path. Plasma hunters and void comets stalk you through the dark, drawn to your warmth, desperate to extinguish what little light remains.

But you are not powerless. Every spark you reclaim is a piece of your star reborn. Every heartbeat you survive takes you deeper into the Emberstorm — the raging edge of the void where Solari's fragments are densest, and the danger is greatest.

Ancient stellar powers await those brave enough to push further: shields forged from nebula dust, time-bending fields that slow the storm, and the legendary Phoenix Heart — the mythical ability to reignite after death itself.

How far can you carry the flame?
